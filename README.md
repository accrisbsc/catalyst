# 🔐 myco - Your Private, Compressed Log Vault

## 🚀 What is myco?

myco is a **free, secure log vault** that keeps your computer's activity records safe, private, and neatly compressed. Think of it as a highly secure diary for your software—it automatically records what your programs are doing, locks that information with powerful encryption (so only you can read it), and shrinks it down to save space. The best part? It includes a built-in viewer that runs in your web browser, so you can see what's happening at a glance without needing any technical skills.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛡️ Military-Grade Encryption
myco uses the same encryption technology trusted by banks and governments (AES-256-GCM and ChaCha20-Poly1305). Your logs are scrambled into an unreadable code that only you can unlock. No one else—not even tech experts—can peek inside without your permission.

### 📦 Smart Compression
Your logs are automatically compressed using Brotli technology. This means myco takes up less space on your computer, making it perfect for long-term record keeping. You'll store more history without filling up your hard drive.

### 🌐 Built-in Web Viewer
No need for complicated software to read your logs. myco includes a simple web viewer that opens in your regular browser (like Chrome, Edge, or Firefox). You'll see everything in a clean, organized format that's easy to understand.

### ⚡ Zero Dependencies
myco is self-contained. It doesn't require you to install anything else, doesn't depend on other programs, and won't clutter your system with extra toolbars or add-ons. It simply works out of the box.

### 🔄 Real-Time Management
Watch your logs update instantly. As your programs run, myco captures and organizes the information in real time. You can monitor system activity as it happens.
